Dayli support

Help with the Dayli calendar app

Common questions

  • Sign-in code never arrives. Check your spam folder, then try Sign in with Apple, which needs no email.
  • Someone cannot see my events. Events are visible to members of the calendar they are in. Share the calendar's join code from Calendar → Members, or invite them to the single event.
  • Reminders are not showing. Allow notifications for Dayli in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → Dayli → Notifications; Android: Settings → Apps → Dayli → Notifications), and check More → Reminder for the default timing.

Delete your account

In the app, or on the web at daylicalendar.co.uk/app if you no longer have the app: sign in, open More, tap Delete account and confirm. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

This removes your profile, your events, photos, files, friendships, push tokens and Ask Dayli history at once. Calendars you own pass to their longest-standing member, or are deleted if you are the only member. Events in calendars you shared stay with the other members, without your name attached. See the privacy policy for what Dayli stores, and the terms of use for the rules.

Contact

Email hello@daylicalendar.co.uk and say which phone and app version you are using (More shows the version at the bottom). We aim to reply within two working days.
