Dayli support

Help with the Dayli calendar app

Common questions

  • Sign-in code never arrives. Check your spam folder, then try Sign in with Apple, which needs no email.
  • Someone cannot see my events. Events are visible to members of the calendar they are in. Share the calendar's join code from Calendar → Members, or invite them to the single event.
  • Reminders are not showing. Allow notifications for Dayli in iOS Settings, and check More → Reminder for the default timing.
  • Delete my account. More → Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

Contact

Email hello@daylicalendar.co.uk and say which phone and app version you are using (More shows the version at the bottom). We aim to reply within two working days.
