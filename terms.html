Reporting and blocking

You can report any message, comment, photo or story from the app: long-press a message, or use the ⋯ menu on a story or photo, and choose Report. A reported photo or story comes off Dayli at once. You can block any person from a chat's ⋯ menu or from More → Blocked people: they can no longer see your stories or message you, and you no longer see theirs. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, as above.

Stories, messages and photos

  • A story is shown to your friends for 24 hours. It then stays on the day it was posted, in the calendar you chose, for the members of that calendar.
  • Photos you add to a shared calendar are shown to its members and on any photo frame paired with it. The sender or the calendar's owner can remove a photo.
  • Private messages are between the two of you. You can delete your own messages at any time.
  • Blocking, deleting and reporting work as described above, and the Dayli team can remove anything that breaks these terms.
