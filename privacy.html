Dayli privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Dayli is a shared calendar for families and friends. This page says what the app stores, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What Dayli stores

DataWhy
Your email address, or your Apple or Google account identifier, and the name you chooseTo sign you in and show your name to people you share calendars with
Calendars, events, participants, comments, polls, invitation pages and the photos or files you attachThat is the calendar. Shared with the members of each calendar and the people you invite, and nobody else
Your mobile number, asked for when you sign upSo friends who have your number can find you, and so an invitation sent to your number reaches you in Dayli. Only you can see it
Phone numbers from your contacts, only if you allow accessTo show which of your contacts are on Dayli, and to show people you already know under the name saved in your phone. Numbers are checked and not kept; names from your contacts never leave your phone
Photos you send to a shared calendar, and the photo frames paired with itShown to the members of that calendar and on its photo frames, and nobody else. The sender or the calendar's owner can remove a photo; anyone in the calendar can report one, which takes it down at once. A frame shows the calendar's photos and its next week of plans (never "only me" events) until it is disconnected in the app
Stories, and private messages between friendsA story is a photo your friends can see for 24 hours; it then stays on the day it was posted in the calendar you chose. Replies to a story, and messages, are private between the two of you. Blocking someone hides their stories and messages and stops them writing to you; reports go to the Dayli team
Your profile photo, only if you add oneSo people can tell who's who. Anyone with the photo's link can open it, so it is stored under a random link
Messages in event and calendar chats, and anything you report or blockSeen by the people on that event or calendar. Reports go to the Dayli team to review; blocks are private to you
Friends, added by email addressSo you can see when a friend is busy. Friends see only busy and free times, never your event titles, unless you share a calendar with them
Busy times from your phone's own calendars, only if you turn that onSo friends see those as busy too. Only start and end times leave the phone, never titles, places or people
A private calendar link, only if you make oneSo Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook can show your Dayli entries. Those apps read the link on their own schedule; anyone who has the link can see the entries in it. Switch it off in More → Synced calendars and the link stops working
Links to other calendars you choose to follow (Google, Apple, Outlook), and the events they containTo show those events inside Dayli. Only you see them; removing the link removes the events
A home town and its coordinates, only if you set oneTo show the weather on your day view
A push notification token for your device, only if you allow notificationsTo deliver invitations, replies and reminders
What you type or say into Ask Dayli and smart add, and the answerTo turn it into events, and so we can fix mistakes the assistant makes. Voice is turned into text on your phone; only the text is sent

Who else processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, files and sign-in, in a European data centre.
  • DeepSeek reads the text you type into Ask Dayli and smart add, together with the names of people in your calendars, to draft events and answer questions. The suggestion on your home screen sends your first name and a short summary of how busy your day is. No other calendar data is sent.
  • Anthropic (the Claude assistant, in the United States) reads any photo or screenshot you choose to scan, with the names of people in your calendars, to turn it into events. Under Anthropic's commercial terms it is not used to train their models.
  • Open-Meteo receives the coordinates of your home town to return a forecast.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in with those accounts. Expo relays push notifications to Apple and Google.

Dayli does not sell data, does not show adverts, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking libraries. It keeps its own note of when the app is opened (the moment, the platform and the app version, against your account) so we can see how many people use Dayli and whether it is growing; those notes are deleted after 13 months and are never shared.

How long it is kept

As long as you have an account. Deleting your account in the app (More → Delete account) removes your profile, your events, photos, files, friendships, tokens and assistant history at once. Events in calendars you shared stay with the other members, without your name attached.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Deletion is self-service in the app. For anything else, write to the address below and we will answer within a month.

Children

Dayli is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
