Shared

Everyone sees the same plans.

One calendar per household or group. Colours per person, a merged view across all your calendars, and changes appear on everyone's phone the moment they're made.

Your day

A brief, not a list.

Tap a day and Dayli tells you what matters: who's waiting on an answer, what clashes, what the weather's doing. Then the timeline, with a line for right now.

Ask Dayli

Just say it.

"Dinner with Sam Thursday at 7" becomes an event. "What's on tomorrow?", "When am I free this week?", "Move gym to 8" all work too, typed or spoken, in plain English.

Find a time

Skip the group chat.

"90 minutes with Jo and Priya on Tuesday or Thursday evenings" and Dayli lists the slots that work for everyone. Friends only ever share busy or free, never what they're doing.

The big ones

Plan it properly.

Give an event a page with a cover photo, a link, a to-do list and a count-down. Invite people who aren't on Dayli with a link. Add the photos afterwards.

Date polls

Pick a date together.

Offer a few dates, everyone taps yes, no or maybe, and when you close the poll the event is made and everyone is invited.
