Dayli

A calendar you share with the people you make plans with.

Try the demo Support

What it does

  • One calendar per household, friend group or club. Everyone sees the same plans.
  • Say it or type it: "dinner with Sam Thursday at 7" becomes an event.
  • Ask Dayli what's on, when you're free, or to move something.
  • Find a time that works for everyone, from busy and free times only. Nobody sees your event titles.
  • Invite people to a single event with a page, a poll for the date, photos afterwards.
